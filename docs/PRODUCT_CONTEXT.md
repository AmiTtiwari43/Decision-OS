# Decision OS — Product Context

## Vision
Make decisions you can explain. Decision OS transforms subjective, multi-variable choices into structured, transparent Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis models with live sensitivity analysis and defensible decision briefings.

## Target Personas
- **Professionals & Leaders**: Evaluating competing job offers, compensation models, and team expansions.
- **Founders & Executives**: Evaluating company relocation, capital allocation, and strategic partnerships.
- **Engineering Leaders & CTOs**: Selecting foundational tech stacks, cloud providers, and architectural paradigms.
